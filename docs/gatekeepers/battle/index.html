Fleet Battle

Set your fleet's orders, then watch it fight on its own. This is how battles would work in the real game: you set orders when it suits you, and the battle plays out during the overnight resolution. You can only see the enemy's silhouettes until you've fought them once. After that, change your orders and press Rematch to try a counter.

Scouting report

What beats what

TorpedoesDestroyers wreck cruisers and carriers, but they're slow and corvette point defence can shoot them down.
LancesCruiser beams strip shields fast. Frigates and shielded ships suffer.
RailgunsFrigate slugs ignore armour. Good against Varkas and against cruisers once their shields drop.
FightersCarrier wings swarm slow, unscreened fleets. Corvettes shred them.
FormationShips at the front get shot first. Put fragile ships behind a screen.
House shipsEach house has one ship nobody else can build: Bastion, Warden, Q-ship, Lancer, Shade, Arkship or Penitent.
RetreatRetreating ships spool their jump drives for a few seconds, then warp out. An early retreat saves ships for later rounds. Nobody is ever knocked out of the game.
Prototype for the Gatekeepers design draft. Every battle is a deterministic simulation: the same orders and the same seed always give the same result, so a server can run it once and every player watches the same replay. House abilities and house ships are first guesses at balance.
