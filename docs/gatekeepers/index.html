Gatekeepers

An asynchronous strategy game for a group of friends. Prototypes for playtesting.

The jump gates that bind human space are failing, and the Concord that controls them is losing its grip. Seven great houses compete for prestige through fleets, treaties and the support of their own people, while the Dimming darkens gate after gate. Everyone has to cooperate enough to keep the gates alive, but only one house wins.

Each round you give three orders, cast your council vote, negotiate, and set your fleets' formation and doctrine. Battles then play out on their own.
